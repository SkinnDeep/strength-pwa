    Is 2 Minutes Too Long For Rest?
    For heavy compound squats and presses (Goblet Squat, Smith Bench, Split Squat), 2 minutes allows nervous system and cellular ATP energy stores to recover ~95%. This ensures hitting the target reps with disciplined form on subsequent sets.
    Fully recovered earlier? Tap SKIP REST or −15s anytime.
    Timer Notifications: Tap the notification bell in the top header so your iPhone alerts you when rest timers finish, even with the screen off or when outside the app.
